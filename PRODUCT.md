# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Mixed, broader professional network rather than one sharp persona: recruiters and hiring managers, technical peers/engineers who'd evaluate real depth, and general industry contacts (LinkedIn, conferences, referrals). The design needs to hold up whether someone is skimming for thirty seconds or reading closely.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Gabriel Chichi, Analytics Engineer at NHS England. The site's job is to help him land a new or better role: it's a credibility and outreach tool, not just an archive. Success looks like a visitor being impressed enough to reach out (email, LinkedIn) or to take him seriously as a candidate on the strength of the site alone.

## Brand Personality

Precise and technical, dev/terminal-native. Leans directly into the engineering identity rather than softening it for a general audience: monospace-forward touches, structured layout, an interface that reads as built by someone who builds pipelines for a living. Confidence comes from precision and craft, not from corporate polish or loud visual flair.

## Anti-references

- The current site: a generic Bootstrap resume template. This is the exact "too basic" problem driving the overhaul.
- Generic AI-generated SaaS cliches: gradient hero text, identical icon+heading card grids, the hero-metric template.
- Anything that reads as stiff or corporate, like a compliance document or an internal slide deck.

## Design Principles

- Engineer the interface the way you'd engineer a pipeline: precise, structured, no wasted motion.
- Confidence through craft, not decoration. Technical rigor is the visual statement, not gradients or SaaS flourishes.
- Built for skimmers and scrutinizers alike: clear at a glance for a recruiter, substantive enough to hold up under a peer engineer's review.
- Every choice should look intentional and specific to this site, never like it was picked off a template.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA baseline: contrast ratios, full keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support. No additional accommodations required beyond good practice.
